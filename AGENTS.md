# Project Memory & Strategic Master Plan

## 1. Multi-Tenant SaaS Vision: ISite Command
- **Product Name**: **ISite Command** (*Intelligent Site Command*)
- **Parent Company**: **Select Property Solutions, LLC**
- **SaaS Business Model**: Standalone subscriber copy allowing earthwork contractors to subscribe, insert their own logo, phone numbers, labor rates, and branding without altering or exposing T.J. Darley Construction branding.
- **Free Trial Structure**: Allows new contractor subscribers 1 Free Full Project Estimate to experience the complete multi-phase bidding engine prior to selecting a monthly subscription tier.
- **Modular Feature Packaging & Tiered Pricing Architecture**:
  - **Tier 1 (Core Estimator & Bidding)**: Baseline earthwork estimating, multi-phase rate tables, profit sensitivity ledger, white-label client PDF exports.
  - **Tier 2 (Field HR & Timecards)**: Adds mobile crew clock-in, geo-fenced jobsite time tracking, and active employee headcount management.
  - **Tier 3 (Plan Takeoffs & CAD/PDF Interpreter)**: Adds site plan blueprint uploads, automated soil profile parsing, and digital PDF takeoff tools.
  - **Tier 4 (Command Center & Drone Photogrammetry)**: Adds drone surface mesh downloads, 3D cut/fill point cloud comparison against CAD baselines, and live stockpile volume audits.
- **Active Employee Tiering & ACH Billing Protection**: Subscription tiers strictly count **Active Employees** in the HR/Payroll module. Terminated, archived, or inactive personnel are excluded from headcount calculations so contractors are never prematurely bumped into higher ACH billing brackets when historical worker records remain in the system.
- **Zero Financial Data Storage Security Mandate**: To eliminate hacking vulnerability and data breach liability for Select Property Solutions, LLC, contractors, and their customers, **iSite Command NEVER stores, processes, or retains credit card numbers, bank account details, or PINs**. All ACH and wire remittances take place bank-to-bank externally with unique automated client reference codes.
- **Official Brand Emblem**: A 3D gold feather quill crossed with a 3D gold lightning bolt in an X shape, set over a dark steel heavy industrial medallion plate with yellow jobsite dust atmosphere — representing speed (lightning), legal proposal precision (quill), and heavy earthwork equipment authority.
- **Official Brand Tagline**: *"Taking Command and Control of your Earthwork Construction Project."*
- **Client Marketing Content**: Uses the high-impact "Earthwork Contractor Tip" educational campaign framework (*"Work ON it—not just IN it"*, *"Know the site, know the quantities, know where the dirt is going"*) to generate social ads, lead magnets, and customer conversion assets.

## 2. Floating AI Assistant Security
- **Access Rule**: The floating TJD AI Assistant Widget (`TjdAiAssistantWidget.tsx`) is locked strictly to authenticated office staff (`tjd_staff_authorized === 'true'` via Office Hub passcode `2026`). It is removed from public view to protect operational knowledge and bid guidance from competitors.

## 3. Estimator Profit Architecture: Markup vs. Margin
- **Markup Formula**: `Profit / Direct Raw Cost` (Calculates dollar markup added to baseline expenses).
- **Margin Formula**: `Profit / Final Selling Price` (Calculates true percentage of total bid retained as profit).
- **Office Copy PDF & Internal Ledger**: Features a live audit matrix displaying:
  - Total Direct Raw Cost
  - Gross Profit Dollars
  - Applied Markup Percentage
  - Actual Net Margin Percentage
  - **Sensitivity Zones**:
    - `< 15% Net Margin`: Aggressive / Low Cushion Zone
    - `15% – 28% Net Margin`: Optimal Competitive & Profitable Zone
    - `> 28% Net Margin`: Premium Yield Zone

## 4. Legal Disclaimer & Estimator Liability Shield
- **Core Principle**: Software provided by Select Property Solutions, LLC (*iSite Command*) serves strictly as an estimating assistance, calculation, and white-label management tool.
- **Contractor Responsibility Mandate**: The subscribing Contractor is solely and exclusively responsible for independently verifying all site dimensions, cut/fill quantities, soil compaction factors, heavy fleet production rates, fuel-burn assumptions, subcontractor quotes, and final proposal prices prior to submitting bids or executing contracts.
- **Liability Exemption**: Neither Select Property Solutions, LLC nor iSite Command shall be liable for any financial losses, underestimations, bidding inaccuracies, site variances, or project cost overruns incurred by the Contractor.

## 5. Contractor Feedback & Criticism Repository
- **Feedback Engine**: Features a dedicated, built-in feedback and criticism repository allowing contractors to submit suggestions, bug reports, calculation feedback, and feature requests.
- **Routing & Storage**: Entries are persisted in local application memory and routed directly to `info@isitecommand.com` and `subscribe@isitecommand.com`.
